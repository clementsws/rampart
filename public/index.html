How to play

1. Choose a castle. Tap one of the castles in your land. A wall is built around it for you.

2. Place cannons inside your walls. Each castle you hold gives you a cannon each round (your home castle gives two).

3. Fire! Tap where you want a cannonball to land. Balls are lobbed and slow (long shots fly high and take a while), so make every shot count and lead moving ships. Knock holes in enemy walls and wreck their cannons.

4. Build & repair. Wall pieces appear. Drag anywhere to slide the piece, tap it to drop it, tap elsewhere to jump it there. ⟳ or a two-finger tap rotates. Close a wall loop around castles to claim them.

Fill craters. Missed shots leave craters that block building for a couple of rounds. During each repair phase you can tap 3 craters in your land to shovel them flat (⛏️).

Anything not fully enclosed after combat is lost, along with its cannons.

Lose all your castles and you're out. Campaign: survive 6 levels of ships arriving in waves (or see how long you last in the Endless Siege). Battle: last player standing, or the highest score after the final round, wins — and decides the losers' fate. Tomatoes, anyone?

Desktop: mouse to aim/move, click to place/fire/fill, right-click or R to rotate, F fills a crater under the piece, arrow keys + space also work.
